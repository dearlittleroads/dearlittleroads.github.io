The Android app

Check the installed version in Settings. Describing version 1.0.1 here does not announce its availability; Google Play shows which version is available to you.

Campaign and Mailbag progress, best move counts, language, music, sound, vibration, and local access state stay on your device. Dear Little Roads has no player account and no cloud save. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may remove puzzle progress.

Version 1.0.1 does not collect or send analytics or diagnostic events. It shows no diagnostics prompt, toggle, identifier, or deletion code. After an update from an earlier test version, old diagnostics settings, random codes, and queued events may remain stored on the device. Version 1.0.1 leaves those separate old records inactive: it does not intentionally read, change, delete, retry access to, use, or send them. An old diagnostics choice inside the ordinary on-device progress record may be passively carried forward unchanged when that record is loaded or saved; it never activates analytics. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local state, but may also remove puzzle progress.

Older test versions for invited testers

Older invitation-only test versions distributed through Google Play—including 0.13.6 and 0.13.7—are different from version 1.0.1. They may still show their older optional diagnostics control. After a tester opted in, those builds could create a random app-profile code, random session/event/route-run codes, and allowlisted gameplay, settings, help, and coarse technical-error events. They did not deliberately include a name, email, Google account, advertising or hardware identifier, contact, location, purchase data, payment data, message, drawn route, or typed text in those events.

The dedicated PostHog Cloud EU project for those builds, including its stored event data, was permanently deleted and verified inaccessible on 11 August 2026. No replacement analytics project is connected to those versions. An installed copy may still retain its consent choice, random code, or queued events locally. While Beta diagnostics remains on, it may send an HTTPS request whose body contains queued allowlisted event records and random identifiers described above to the retired endpoint. PostHog infrastructure can also receive ordinary network metadata. There is no active project for the old event rows, but the request may still reach PostHog infrastructure and we cannot promise when transient request data is deleted. Use the diagnostics control shown by your build: in version 0.13.6, open About; in version 0.13.7, open Settings; then switch Beta diagnostics off. This withdraws the earlier opt-in, stops new attempts, and clears the pending queue. Version 1.0.1 never sends this old data and does not intentionally access or delete the separate old diagnostics records; they may remain inactive until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. An old diagnostics choice inside the ordinary progress record may be carried forward unchanged but is never acted on.

Optional internal consent and demo-ad test

This section applies only to the separately labelled diagnostic test app used privately by the developer and specifically invited adult test operators. It is not the released Dear Little Roads game, a Google Play rollout or a purchase. Participation is voluntary. Before any Google test service is contacted, we ask the test operator to review this notice and agree to that diagnostic run. This consent is separate from the choices in Google's message; declining the test does not affect access to the existing game.

The first stage uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to retrieve and display the configured privacy message and check changes to choices. This contacts Google before a choice can be made in that message: Google receives the connection's IP address and information identifying the app and consent request. UMP stores choice signals on the test device. Refusing advertising does not undo that initial privacy-service connection. The initial diagnostic host is UMP-only: it contains no Google advertising SDK dependency or demo-ad implementation.

A later, separately reviewed and authorized diagnostic build may add an optional second stage that initializes Google's advertising SDK and requests an official Google demo interstitial. This stage is not available in the initial UMP-only host and also requires the test's current consent and legitimate-interest checks to permit the intended mode. It never uses our live ad unit. Demo ads do not charge advertisers; they are not proof of live advertising, revenue or an ad-supported game release. Initialization and demo requests are real Google-service connections, not offline simulations. Google's SDK can process IP address and approximate location inferred from it, interactions, diagnostics and device/app-related or applicable account-related identifiers for advertising services, provider analytics and fraud prevention. Test ads and non-personalized ads are not data-free. No separate developer gameplay-analytics service is added.

Google's message presents the applicable partners, purposes and choices. Its common-partner list can change; the list does not mean every partner receives every test request. Personalization and ordinary-ad device storage require consent. Where the message identifies legitimate-interest processing, you can object. Declining personalization permits a non-personalized demo request only if the remaining requirements are met. Refusal, closing without agreement, missing required permission or an unqualified result means no new advertising-SDK initialization or ad request.

Use Privacy choices in the diagnostic app to change choices, withdraw advertising consent or object; close the diagnostic app to stop starting further diagnostic actions and withdraw participation. Closing does not cancel already-started SDK work, in-flight network requests or processing of data already received by Google. You can also contact us using the details on this page. Withdrawal does not undo previous processing or instantly dismiss a visible ad. Removing the diagnostic app or its data removes its local choices, not Google's server-side records. We retain only redacted technical test results needed to verify this integration; we do not export complete consent strings or device/account identifiers into those results. Results are removed when no longer needed for that verification. Google's handling, retention and international processing are described in the Google Privacy Policy and information about partner apps; we cannot promise a Google deletion date or EU-only processing. The controller, contact and rights sections elsewhere on this page also apply to our handling of this test.

Gmail is a separate Google service and may process this notification outside the EU on our instruction under Google's own terms.
