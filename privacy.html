Closed-beta privacy policy

Your route is yours.

Effective: 21 July 2026 · Developer: Lantern Lion Games · Contact: dear.little.roads@gmail.com

This policy covers the self-join Dear Little Roads Android closed beta, the optional in-game diagnostics, and this website. The game remains fully playable if you decline diagnostics.

Who controls your data

The data controller for Dear Little Roads is Lisa Wegel, operating under the developer name Lantern Lion Games.

Privacy, support, and deletion requests can be sent to dear.little.roads@gmail.com.

This website

This information site uses no website analytics, advertising trackers, account system, or non-essential cookies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages because we have a legitimate interest in providing a secure and reliable information page. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in. We do not receive those security logs. GitHub processes this information under its privacy statement and may process it outside the European Economic Area using the safeguards described there.

What stays on your device

Campaign progress, best move counts, Mailbag progress, language choice, and sound and vibration preferences stay on your device. Dear Little Roads has no player account and does not use cloud saves in this beta.

Optional beta diagnostics

Dear Little Roads sends no analytics until you explicitly choose Share diagnostics. You may decline and still use the whole game, or withdraw your consent later in About.

When enabled, the app sends a random app-profile code; random session and route-run codes; the app version and beta cohort; timestamps and local date; allowlisted puzzle events such as starts, completions, moves, stamps, hints, resets, and exits; active-time totals; Mailbag progress; settings state; sharing actions and views of completed routes; and coarse technical-error categories.

Diagnostic event properties do not include your name, email, Google account, contacts, messages, precise or coarse location, Advertising ID, Android ID, route drawing, individual taps, clipboard, typed text, purchases, or health data. They also do not deliberately include a device model or full user agent. Like any HTTPS service, PostHog may transiently process ordinary network metadata, including an IP address and WebView user-agent header, to receive a request.

Why and where diagnostics are processed

Diagnostics help us understand onboarding, puzzle difficulty, voluntary return, Mailbag use, and coarse technical reliability during the beta. The legal basis is your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

Allowlisted events are sent over HTTPS to a dedicated PostHog Cloud EU project hosted in Frankfurt. PostHog acts as our analytics service provider and publishes its own privacy notice. Dear Little Roads uses no PostHog SDK, autocapture, or session replay. Every event sets $process_person_profile: false and $geoip_disable: true. PostHog is configured not to store the client IP with events, although it may process the IP transiently before discarding it.

Beta access and support email

The closed beta has no application form or approval queue. Access is administered through a publicly discoverable, self-join Google Group connected to the closed Google Play test. When you join, Google records your group membership. As group owner, we can see the Google-account email address and profile name associated with that membership. Under the group permissions, other testers cannot view the member list, conversations, or member email addresses. The group's name, address, and description are public, and anyone on the web can join while recruitment is open.

We use the membership record only to grant and administer Google Play test access, send essential test or safety information if needed, and respond to your messages. Posting and conversations are restricted to the group owner; the group is not used for discussion or advertising. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in safely operating and evaluating a small closed test under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Participation is voluntary, and you may leave the group and Play test at any time.

Google processes your account, test enrolment, download, device, and service information under its privacy policy when you use Google Play. Gmail processes messages you send us. Google may process data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards described in its data-transfer frameworks.

Your group membership remains until you leave or the group is closed. Within 30 days after the relevant beta cohort ends, we close the group or remove any remaining members and delete any separately exported organizer-controlled tester-access records. Support and privacy-request correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations. Google may retain its own service, security, account, or backup records under its policies.

Retention and deletion of diagnostics

We delete raw beta diagnostic events no later than 60 days after the relevant beta cohort begins, after recording aggregate product decisions. Turning diagnostics off stops new collection and clears events waiting on the device.

After opting in, About displays a random deletion code. To delete already uploaded events, turn diagnostics off and send that exact code to dear.little.roads@gmail.com. Keep diagnostics off and do not reuse the code after requesting deletion. We will request deletion from PostHog and confirm only after the matching raw events are absent; PostHog may process event deletion on a schedule.

Sharing, sale, and advertising

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. PostHog, GitHub, and Google process the service and network information described above under our configuration and their applicable terms.

Security and your choices

Diagnostics are encrypted in transit. Access to the beta analytics project and Google Group member list is restricted to the organizer. No storage method is perfectly secure, so the beta deliberately minimizes what it collects.

You may decline diagnostics, withdraw consent, switch diagnostics off, request deletion, object to tester-list processing, leave the Google Group and Play test, or uninstall at any time. None of these choices affects ordinary support.

Children

This measured beta invites adults aged 18 or older and is not directed to children. A future public release will be reviewed separately before changing the target audience.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, and receive portable data where applicable. You may withdraw diagnostics consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before it. Contact dear.little.roads@gmail.com. You may also complain to a data-protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes materially for a later cohort, the updated policy will show a new effective date. The diagnostic consent and data inventory will not be expanded silently during a cohort.

Freiwillige Beta-Diagnose

Dear Little Roads sendet keine Analysedaten, bevor du ausdrücklich Diagnosedaten teilen auswählst. Du kannst ablehnen und trotzdem das gesamte Spiel nutzen. Deine Einwilligung kannst du später unter „Über“ widerrufen.

Wenn die Diagnose aktiviert ist, sendet die App einen zufälligen App-Profilcode; zufällige Sitzungs- und Routendurchlaufcodes; App-Version und Betakohorte; Zeitstempel und lokales Datum; ausschließlich zugelassene Puzzle-Ereignisse wie Starts, Abschlüsse, Züge, Stempel, Hinweise, Zurücksetzen und Verlassen; aktive Spielzeit; Fortschritt in der Posttasche; Einstellungsstatus; Teilen und Anzeigen abgeschlossener Routen sowie grobe Kategorien technischer Fehler.

Die Diagnoseereignisse enthalten nicht deinen Namen, deine E-Mail-Adresse oder dein Google-Konto, Kontakte, Nachrichten, einen genauen oder ungefähren Standort, Werbe-ID, Android-ID, eingezeichnete Route, einzelne Berührungen, Zwischenablage, eingegebenen Text, Käufe oder Gesundheitsdaten. Gerätemodell und vollständiger User-Agent werden ebenfalls nicht gezielt in die Diagnoseereignisse aufgenommen. Wie jeder HTTPS-Dienst kann PostHog beim Empfang einer Anfrage gewöhnliche Netzwerkdaten wie IP-Adresse und WebView-User-Agent vorübergehend verarbeiten.

Warum und wo Diagnosedaten verarbeitet werden

Die Diagnose hilft uns zu verstehen, wie der Einstieg gelingt, wie schwierig die Rätsel sind, ob Spielende von sich aus zurückkehren, wie sie die Posttasche nutzen und ob grobe technische Probleme auftreten. Rechtsgrundlage ist deine Einwilligung gemäß Art. 6 Abs. 1 Buchst. a DSGVO.

Die zugelassenen Ereignisse werden verschlüsselt an ein eigenes PostHog-Cloud-EU-Projekt in Frankfurt übertragen. PostHog ist unser Analysedienstleister und veröffentlicht eine eigene Datenschutzerklärung. Dear Little Roads verwendet kein PostHog-SDK, kein Autocapture und keine Sitzungsaufzeichnung. Jedes Ereignis setzt $process_person_profile: false und $geoip_disable: true. PostHog ist so konfiguriert, dass die IP-Adresse nicht zusammen mit Ereignissen gespeichert wird; beim Empfang kann sie kurzzeitig verarbeitet und anschließend verworfen werden.

Aufbewahrung und Löschung von Diagnosedaten

Rohe Beta-Diagnosedaten löschen wir spätestens 60 Tage nach Beginn der jeweiligen Betakohorte, nachdem wir daraus nur zusammengefasste Erkenntnisse und Produktentscheidungen festgehalten haben. Das Ausschalten der Diagnose stoppt die weitere Erfassung und löscht noch nicht übertragene Ereignisse auf deinem Gerät.

Nach deiner Einwilligung zeigt „Über“ einen zufälligen Löschcode an. Um bereits übertragene Ereignisse zu löschen, schalte die Diagnose aus und sende genau diesen Code an dear.little.roads@gmail.com. Lass die Diagnose danach ausgeschaltet und verwende den Code nicht erneut. Wir veranlassen die Löschung bei PostHog und bestätigen sie erst, wenn die passenden Rohdaten nicht mehr vorhanden sind; PostHog verarbeitet Löschaufträge möglicherweise zeitversetzt.
