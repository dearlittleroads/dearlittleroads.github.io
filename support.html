Beta support

Something went off route?

Dear Little Roads support is operated by Lisa Wegel under the developer name Lantern Lion Games. Contact dear.little.roads@gmail.com. Please include the Dear Little Roads version shown in About, your Android version if known, what you expected, and what happened.

Do not send passwords, payment details, contacts, private photos, or unrelated device logs. Dear Little Roads support will never ask for your Google password.

Withdraw an application or beta record

Email dear.little.roads@gmail.com from the Google-account email entered in the application and ask to withdraw or delete the recruitment record. You may do this before or after selection. We will remove the organizer-controlled application, invitation, first-open, selection-note, and tester-list records that can be matched to that address, except for any minimal record temporarily needed to resolve and evidence the request. Never send your Google password.

The unlinked Day-8 survey does not ask for an email or response identifier. If you want us to try to locate one survey response before the raw-response deletion deadline, include its approximate submission time and enough answer detail to distinguish it; do not add new identifying information merely for this purpose.

Beta data deletion

If diagnostics were enabled, open About → Data details, switch Beta diagnostics off, copy the random deletion code, and email that exact code with the subject Delete Dear Little Roads beta data. Do not include your Google password or device identifiers. Keep diagnostics off and do not reuse the code after requesting deletion. We will confirm only after the matching PostHog events are absent; PostHog may process event deletion on a schedule.

Turn diagnostics off

Open About and switch Beta diagnostics off. This immediately stops new collection and clears events waiting to upload. It does not remove progress or disable game features.

Leave the beta

Use the original Google Play opt-in page, choose to leave the test, and uninstall normally. Leaving is always voluntary.

Known beta boundaries

  • Android 7.0 or newer is required.
  • Progress is stored on the device and may be lost after uninstalling, clearing app data, or a later beta reset.
  • There is no account or cross-device restore.
  • The beta has no ads, purchases, or paid support.
