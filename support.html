Something went off route?

Dear Little Roads support is operated by Lisa Wegel under the developer name Lantern Lion Games. Contact dear.little.roads@gmail.com.

For a technical problem, include the Dear Little Roads version shown in About, your Android version if known, what you expected, and what happened. The form does not accept attachments; if a screenshot would help and contains no private information, use the email fallback.

Do not send passwords, payment details, contacts, private photos, purchase tokens, or unrelated device logs. Dear Little Roads support will never ask for your Google password.

Contact support

Nothing is sent while you read or type. When you press Send message, your reply email, selected topic, message, and the form language are submitted to our contact-form provider, Formward. Formward then attempts to deliver a notification to our Gmail support inbox. See the privacy policy for providers, retention, and your rights.

Please include only what we need to answer. Do not send passwords, payment credentials, purchase tokens, or unrelated private information.

If the form does not work, email dear.little.roads@gmail.com.

The form service returned you after processing the submission; mailbox delivery is not confirmed. Use the email link if needed.

Your version and earlier purchases

Check your installed version in Settings. The version 1.0.1 or 1.0.2 information below applies when that version is installed; this page does not announce update availability. Google Play shows which version is available to you.

Version 1.0.1 or 1.0.2 has no ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. All 100 campaign routes and the full Mailbag/archive are available through normal earned progression. You do not need to buy or restore a purchase to play them.

Earlier full-unlock purchases remain associated with the Google Play account that acquired them; this update does not cancel them. The free version does not check or restore purchase ownership, including after a fresh installation. Existing local access records are left untouched. Puzzle progress is separate and stays only on the device; Google Play does not restore it.

Version 1.0.0 used a purchase for Routes 11–63 and the full Mailbag/archive. Its Restore purchase control uses the Google account that made the purchase; a pending payment does not count as confirmed ownership. If an earlier purchase is pending, refunded, or otherwise looks wrong, contact us. We may ask for the Google Play order ID or, only if it does not locate the order, the full Google-account email used for the purchase. Never send payment credentials, passwords, or a purchase token. We keep no routine order export or separate order database; the privacy policy explains the limited support and retention rules.

Privacy and diagnostics

Version 1.0.1 or 1.0.2 has no analytics or diagnostics collection, prompt, toggle, identifier, or deletion code. After an update from an earlier test version, separate old diagnostics settings, random codes, and queued events may remain on the device, inactive. Version 1.0.1 or 1.0.2 does not intentionally read, change, delete, retry access to, use, or send them. An old diagnostics choice inside the ordinary progress record may be passively carried forward unchanged but never activates analytics. See the privacy policy for the limited device, Google Play, website, email, and closed-test data that can still be involved.

Older invitation-only test versions distributed through Google Play—including 0.13.6 and 0.13.7—are different. They may still show the older optional diagnostics control and, after an earlier opt-in, retain a random diagnostics code and queued events on the device. While Beta diagnostics remains on, an installed copy may send an HTTPS request containing queued allowlisted event records and random identifiers to the retired PostHog Cloud EU endpoint. PostHog infrastructure may also receive ordinary network metadata. The dedicated service project and its stored data were permanently deleted on 11 August 2026, and no replacement project is connected to those test builds, but the request may still reach PostHog infrastructure. Use the diagnostics control shown by your build: in version 0.13.6, open About; in version 0.13.7, open Settings; then switch Beta diagnostics off. This withdraws the earlier opt-in, stops new attempts, and clears the pending queue. Do not send us the old deletion code: there is no remaining project record to match it to. Version 1.0.1 or 1.0.2 never sends the old local data and leaves separate old diagnostics records untouched and inactive. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local state, but can remove puzzle progress.

If you joined an invitation-only closed test

Open the Google Play test page, choose to leave the test, and uninstall normally. Leaving is always voluntary.

Leaving the Play test does not necessarily remove your address from our organizer-managed tester list. To request removal, email dear.little.roads@gmail.com from the Google-account email used for test access.

Known limits

  • Android 7.0 or newer is required.
  • Progress is stored on the device and may be lost after uninstalling or clearing app data.
  • There is no player account or cloud save. Keep the app installed and update it without clearing data to retain local progress.
  • Version 1.0.1 or 1.0.2 has no ads, in-app purchases, or subscriptions; support is free.

Datenschutz und Diagnose

Version 1.0.1 oder 1.0.2 enthält keine Analyse- oder Diagnoseerfassung, keine Diagnoseabfrage, keinen Diagnoseschalter, keine Diagnosekennung und keinen Löschcode. Nach einer Aktualisierung von einer früheren Testfassung können getrennte alte Diagnoseeinstellungen, zufällige Kennungen und wartende Ereignisse inaktiv auf dem Gerät gespeichert bleiben. Version 1.0.1 oder 1.0.2 liest, ändert oder löscht sie nicht absichtlich und versucht auch später nicht erneut, auf sie zuzugreifen; sie verwendet ihren Inhalt nicht und überträgt ihn nicht. Eine alte Diagnoseentscheidung im gewöhnlichen Spielstandsdatensatz kann passiv und unverändert mitgeführt werden, löst aber keine Analyse aus. Die Datenschutzerklärung beschreibt die begrenzten Geräte-, Google-Play-, Website-, E-Mail- und Testdaten, die dennoch betroffen sein können.

Die älteren, über Google Play nur an eingeladene Testpersonen verteilten Testfassungen – darunter die Versionen 0.13.6 und 0.13.7 – sind anders. Sie können noch den älteren optionalen Diagnoseschalter anzeigen und nach einer früheren Einwilligung eine zufällige Diagnosekennung und wartende Ereignisse auf dem Gerät speichern. Bei eingeschalteter Beta-Diagnose kann eine installierte Fassung eine HTTPS-Anfrage mit wartenden freigegebenen Ereignisdatensätzen und zufälligen Kennungen an den stillgelegten PostHog-Cloud-EU-Endpunkt senden. Die PostHog-Infrastruktur kann außerdem gewöhnliche Netzwerk-Metadaten erhalten. Das zugehörige Dienstprojekt und seine gespeicherten Daten wurden am 11. August 2026 dauerhaft gelöscht; mit diesen Testfassungen ist kein Ersatzprojekt verbunden. Die Anfrage kann die PostHog-Infrastruktur weiterhin erreichen. Verwende den Diagnoseschalter deiner Fassung: Öffne in Version 0.13.6 „Über“ beziehungsweise in Version 0.13.7 „Einstellungen“ und schalte die Beta-Diagnose aus. Dadurch widerrufst du die frühere Einwilligung, stoppst neue Versuche und löschst die Warteschlange. Sende uns den alten Löschcode nicht: Es gibt keinen verbliebenen Projektdatensatz, dem er zugeordnet werden könnte. Version 1.0.1 oder 1.0.2 sendet die alten lokalen Daten nie und lässt getrennte alte Diagnosedatensätze unangetastet und inaktiv. Beim Löschen der App-Daten oder bei einer Deinstallation werden lokal gespeicherte Daten entfernt; dabei kann auch der Rätselfortschritt verloren gehen.
